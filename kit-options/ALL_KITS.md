# ProjectG: kit options

55 alternatives / 275 skills. 36 straightforward kits; 19 signature variants.

Short effects for comparison. See FULL_RULES.md for targeting, timing and edge cases, and ABILITY_CATALOGUE.md for mechanics and synergy notes. Values are preliminary.
