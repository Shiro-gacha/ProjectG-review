Roselle Vey · Roseguard Sovereign

Your D / E / D / D / E mix, rebuilt with your notes and the allied-shield interaction.

Kit proposal for your review. Your saved selections and notes are preserved. This is not a playable roster addition.

Overall: A / A+Protection: A+Prepared burst ceiling: S / S+Boss support: A

Roselle is a strong defensive hybrid. Her ordinary shields provide modest bonus damage; reaching the full 35% Max HP bonus requires a large shield pool and coordinated setup. That prepared burst can rival dedicated attackers while she still brings protection and cleansing. Her provisional rating is A/A+ in ordinary teams, with an S/S+ burst ceiling; the ceiling needs encounter testing.

Shared Break rule: Basic 0 · Active 1 · Ultimate 2. Elemental advantage adds 1. Only successful offensive hits count, once per target per action; pure support and triggered reactions add none.

Ultimate ceiling: a shield pool equal to 70% of Roselle's Max HP is needed for the 35% bonus. Ordinary casts deal less. The cost is paid once and its damage bonus applies to each enemy hit.

Passive · Automatic

After the Bubble Breaks

Self and shielded allies

Increase Roselle's Max HP by 12%.

When enemy damage breaks an ally's shield, heal that ally for 4% of Roselle's Max HP. Up to twice during each unit's turn.

Detailed rules

Increase Roselle's Max HP by 12%. When direct enemy damage depletes a temporary HP shield from any hero on a living ally, including Roselle, heal that ally for 4% of Roselle's Max HP after the enemy action resolves. Trigger once per recipient per enemy action and at most twice during each unit's turn. The shared allowance is available at wave entry and resets when any ally or enemy begins a scheduled turn; reactions do not create turns or refresh it. If more than two allies qualify, prioritize lowest remaining HP percentage, then formation slot. Roselle must be alive. Defeated recipients are not healed. Expiry, dispel, DoTs, friendly damage and voluntary shield consumption do not trigger it.

Accepts any hero's HP shields. Two heals are shared across the team in each unit's turn, not two per ally or two per damage component.

Basic · No cooldown

Droplet Knuckle

One enemy and self

Deal 90% ATK damage.

Shield Roselle for 4% of her Max HP for 2 turns.

Detailed rules

Deal 90% of Roselle's ATK damage, then give Roselle a shield equal to 4% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 of her turns. A newly created shield cannot enable A Crown Under Pressure on this same action.

Provides a personal HP shield without a resource passive.

Active 1 · CD 2

Shelter One Rose

One ally

Shield one ally for 11% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 turns.

Increase that ally's DEF by 20% for 2 turns.

Detailed rules

Give the selected living ally a shield equal to 11% of Roselle's Max HP and 20% DEF for 2 recipient turns. The shield refreshes to the higher remaining/new value; generic DEF buffs use the strongest value rather than adding together.

Focused shield and DEF increase; it does not directly heal.

Active 2 · CD 3

Pearl-Lined Canopy

All allies

Shield all allies for 6% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 turns.

Remove 1 debuff from every ally.

Detailed rules

Give every living ally a shield equal to 6% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 recipient turns, then remove the oldest removable debuff from each of them. Each ally is checked independently. If an ally has no removable debuff, their shield still applies.

Full-party protection and one cleanse per ally; independent of passive triggers.

Ultimate · CD 5 · 100 charge

Crown in a Thousand Drops

All enemies and self

Deal 125% ATK damage to all enemies.

Consume shields on Roselle from any hero. Add 50% of the amount consumed as damage per enemy, capped at 35% of her Max HP.

Then shield Roselle for 8% of her Max HP for 2 turns.

Detailed rules

At cast start snapshot Roselle's current Max HP, including her passive. Consume temporary HP shields currently on Roselle from any hero, up to a total of 70% of that Max HP; preserve excess shielding. Consume layers with the earliest expiry first, then application order. Spend once for the whole cast, never once per enemy. For each enemy hit, deal 125% of Roselle's ATK plus 50% of the shield HP consumed, with the bonus capped at 35% of her snapshotted Max HP before standard DEF, type and critical modifiers. This is direct damage, not true damage. With no shield the base attack still works. Named guard charges such as Frostplate do not count as HP shields. After the attack, shield Roselle for 8% of her Max HP for 2 recipient turns; this new shield cannot fund the same cast. Voluntary consumption never triggers shield-break healing. The shared Ultimate Break budget applies only once per enemy hit. Distinct named shield layers can coexist; the same named shield refreshes to the larger remaining/new value rather than stacking. Existing runtime shields do not currently provide these source layers.

Allied shield providers can fuel this attack. Only shields on Roselle are spent; allies keep their own shields. A 70% Max HP shield pool is needed for the 35% bonus ceiling.

How strong is she?

What makes her strong

  • One stat, Max HP, improves her durability, every shield she grants, shield-break recovery and the Ultimate's bonus ceiling.
  • Shielding all five allies while cleansing one debuff from each is reliable utility without an Accuracy check or private stack meter.
  • Shield-break recovery can help during enemy turns and works with allied shields; the new per-turn limit allows more recovery across multiple enemy actions than the earlier twice-per-Roselle-turn version.
  • Ultimate converts already-prepared defense into area pressure while preserving excess shield and rebuilding a small self-shield.

What holds her back

  • Two selected Actives deal no damage. Without a large stored shield pool, her damage and boss-breaking cadence lag behind dedicated attackers.
  • The Ultimate removes defense before dealing damage. Its new 8% self-shield may be much smaller than the pool spent, leaving her exposed afterward.
  • The passive needs enemy damage to break a shield. Expiry, dispel, voluntary consumption and damage over time do not trigger it; one enemy area action can heal only two recipients.
  • One cleanse per ally is useful but cannot clear several debuffs at once. Her direct party healing is conditional, and she has no resurrection, Provoke or allied intercept.
  • Shield refresh rules, two-turn expiry, enemy shield removal and turn order can prevent the large pool from surviving until her Ultimate.
  • DEF +20% is not 20% damage reduction: at DEF 200 under the current ordinary damage formula it reduces subsequent neutral direct damage by about 10.9%.

Assumptions, sources and limits
  • Selected slots are D Passive / E Basic / D Active 1 / D Active 2 / E Ultimate. Roselle's final stat line, Speed and investment are not yet defined, so no precise position among 29 runtime heroes or ten other proposed heroes is defensible.
  • Passive: Max HP +12%. Enemy damage breaking any hero's temporary shield on a surviving ally heals that ally for 4% of Roselle's final Max HP, at most once per recipient per enemy action and twice per acting unit's turn. The effect cannot restore a defeated ally.
  • Active 1 gives one ally a shield worth 11% of Roselle's Max HP and 20% DEF for two recipient turns, CD 2. Active 2 gives all living allies 6% of her Max HP in shields and removes one removable debuff from each, CD 3.
  • Ultimate: 125% ATK to each enemy plus half of the shields actually consumed from Roselle, from any hero. Consume at most 70% of her Max HP; bonus caps at 35% of her Max HP per enemy. Excess shield is retained. Afterward she receives an 8%-Max-HP self-shield. Remove the obsolete 160%-ATK total cap.
  • Assume damage receives ordinary DEF/type/critical rules. The examples use the proposal's listed damage coefficients directly before mitigation and do not apply a second legacy area penalty. Shields and healing cannot crit.
  • Cross-source shield stacking must be explicitly supported for the proposed design. Current runtime has one shared Shield value; AddPercentShield refreshes to the larger value, so ordinary barriers from several heroes do not automatically add together.
  • Boss assessment assumes the user's new break direction: neutral damaging Basic/Active/Ultimate contribute 0/1/2, elemental advantage adds 1, and content gauges are being expanded to 20–40+. Non-damaging protection skills do not deal break.
  • Existing-peer stat examples are equal level 20, three stars, skill level 1, no gear, training, memories, familiars or archive bonuses, in default rows, before battle-start passives. These examples illustrate ratios rather than claim an actual account build.
  • Qualitative design review only: no Roselle runtime implementation, final stats, encounter simulation or measured win-rate comparison has been supplied.
  • No invented exact rank among all roster heroes. Existing and proposed peers are distinct; the other ten concepts have alternative kits rather than a final selected roster.
  • Older runtime shields persist until consumed; proposed barriers expire after two recipient turns. Older and proposed skills also use different coefficient conventions and status/cleanse rules.
  • Skill upgrades can reduce runtime cooldowns at skill level 5 and increase Power, while fixed-percent protections often remain hardcoded. Compare equal investment and finalized rules before balancing.
  • Changing the shield pool's stacking model, consumption order or duration changes both the reachable ceiling and survivability cost. Those rules are part of the design, not automatic compatibility from similar wording.
  • Voluntary shield conversion cannot trigger Roselle's passive heal or any on-damage/charge reaction. The cap limits damage, not a free heal-and-refill loop.
