Combat rules & how to read the numbers

Review scope. These are alternative kit designs for your choice or mixing, not implemented or balance-tested skills. Suggested types and roles are also proposals. Visual acceptance is separate from gameplay acceptance.

Simple wording. Cards show the core effect and its main limits. Detailed rules and targeting are expandable. Per-turn trigger limits reset at the start of the named hero's turn and are available from wave entry. Straightforward kits use familiar effects without private resource or mark engines; signature variants retain selected distinctive mechanics.

Slots and charge. Five-unit parties use a persistent Speed/action gauge. Passive is automatic; Basic has no cooldown; Active 1 has CD2; Active 2 has CD3; Ultimate has CD5 and costs 100 charge. Basic gives 18 charge and each Active gives 26 once per action. Charge caps at 100. Ultimate and triggered follow-ups/counters grant no charge. Extra turns and cooldown resets are excluded from these initial options.

Cooldown and duration timing. Cooldowns tick at the start of the caster's subsequent turns before skill selection: CD2 is ready on the second subsequent turn. A turn duration counts the affected unit's subsequent turns and expires at the end of its final counted turn. Effects do not tick or expire in the action that applies them. Damage over time ticks at the start of those turns. Custom rules state exceptions.

Numbers. Damage coefficients mean percent of the caster's ATK before DEF and type mitigation unless explicitly HP/DEF-scaled. Direct action damage can crit; triggered damage cannot unless specified. Healing and shields do not crit. Max HP healing uses the recipient's Max HP unless stated otherwise. Values are preliminary design coefficients, not final engine Power values.

Stacks and shields. Same-named nonstacking buffs and debuffs refresh duration; generic modifiers use the strongest value, not a sum. Shields last two recipient turns unless specified. Same-named shields retain the larger remaining/new value instead of adding together. Custom resources start as defined, are owned by their caster, clear between waves, and have explicit caps. Reactions cannot trigger other reactions or grant charge. Between-turn trigger allowances start unused at wave entry. A defeated caster cannot act or trigger passives; queued earned healing can resolve if its recipient is still alive.

Resistance and control. Hostile statuses, dispels and gauge reductions use Effect Accuracy versus Resistance with base 100% unless lower odds are specified; separate effects check separately once per target per action. Root and Freeze deny the next one action; overlapping action-denial effects merge into that single denial rather than adding skipped actions. After a denied action the target is control-immune through its following actionable turn. Provoke restricts offensive single-target skills for one turn. Charm redirects Basic attacks only.

Break. Successful offensive Basics deal 0 Break, Actives 1 and Ultimates 2. Elemental advantage adds 1, giving 1/2/3. This shared rule is implicit in skill text, applies once per target per chosen action, and does not multiply with damage components or multi-hits. Pure healing, shields, buffs, passives, DoTs and triggered reactions add no Break. No exceptional extra-Break riders are selected in these proposals. Boss capacities scale by content, roughly 20–40+; Break gauge is separate from the 0–100 action gauge.

Bosses and action gauge. Standard bosses are immune to Root, Freeze, Stun, Charm, Provoke and gauge reduction. Every option has a boss plan for its ordinary effects; control immunity does not grant additional Break. Action-gauge points refer to a 0–100 gauge; a 10% push is 10 percentage points. Ordinary debuffs can still be resisted.

Roselle's shields. Every Roselle shield uses Roselle's Max HP at the time it is applied, including shields on allies, resource bonuses and passive caps. The shield snapshots that amount; later Max HP changes do not resize an existing shield. New contributions obey the cap at their own application time and cannot increase a shield already above that cap. Her ATK-based attacks and healing remain explicitly separate. Overheal triggers Rosewater barriers of a fixed Max-HP-based size rather than converting the raw excess amount. This gives durable builds stronger protection without uncapped shield growth.

Mixing. Choose one skill per slot within the same unit, from A through E. ‘Needs an engine’ means a hard dependency is absent. ‘Base effect works’ means only an optional bonus is missing. Checks cover declared dependencies, not every balance interaction; read the mix notes. Each full kit supplies its own declared engine. Exports preserve skill IDs and notes; they do not submit or approve a final kit. Existing saved A–C skill choices and notes are retained when this workshop updates.
