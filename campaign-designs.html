About this interactive study

The three sample chapters use ProjectG stage names, briefing text and Normal-mode numbers. Hard is a visual state preview. All five designs use the same sample party and stages. Battles, cache claims and formation changes here are demonstrations and do not affect a game account. A cleared stage remains a revisit; its first-clear rewards are not granted again.

The full campaign currently contains 15 chapters and 360 stages. These concepts show chapters 1, 2 and 7. The shared prebattle preview uses the approved 3×3 formation, with five heroes; entering its battle preview freezes placement and removes formation controls. Sample stage data.
