About this interactive study

The expanded B campaign and C chapter route share selection, difficulty, sample progress and the prebattle party. The initial five studies remain available for comparison. Story markers identify actual authored scenes; scene names are shown without playing native dialogue. Boss markers come from the existing stage type.

The three sample chapters use ProjectG stage names, briefing text and Normal-mode numbers. Hard is a visual state preview. Normal and Hard Campaign have no energy entry cost. Account energy is reserved for designated farming stages, such as gear farming. Battles, cache claims and formation changes here are demonstrations and do not affect a game account. A cleared stage remains a revisit; its first-clear rewards are not granted again.

The full campaign currently contains 15 chapters and 360 stages. These concepts show chapters 1, 2 and 7. The shared prebattle preview uses the approved 3×3 formation, with five heroes; entering its battle preview freezes placement and removes formation controls.
