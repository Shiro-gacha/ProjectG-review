Roselle Vey

Roseguard Sovereign · User draft D/E/D/D/E

Recommendation · awaiting your choice

Keep the user's assembled draft as the review baseline.

HP-based shields, cleansing and conditional recovery give a clear defensive identity. Shield conversion is the signature exception in this group.

Read the five proposed skills
Passive

After the Bubble Breaks

Increase Roselle's Max HP by 12%.

When enemy damage breaks an ally's shield, heal that ally for 4% of Roselle's Max HP. Up to twice during each unit's turn.

Basic

Droplet Knuckle

Deal 90% ATK damage.

Shield Roselle for 4% of her Max HP for 2 turns.

Active 1

Shelter One Rose

Shield one ally for 11% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 turns.

Increase that ally's DEF by 20% for 2 turns.

Active 2

Pearl-Lined Canopy

Shield all allies for 6% of Roselle's Max HP for 2 turns.

Remove 1 debuff from every ally.

Ultimate

Crown in a Thousand Drops

Deal 125% ATK damage to all enemies.

Consume shields on Roselle from any hero. Add 50% of the amount consumed as damage per enemy, capped at 35% of her Max HP.

Then shield Roselle for 8% of her Max HP for 2 turns.

Tradeoffs & suggested mixing

Tradeoff

Her two support Actives add no Break. Conversion spends her own protection and needs source-layer shields that the current runtime does not support.

Mixing note

Do not replace the saved D/E/D/D/E draft with a pure option. Review the assembled Ultimate's ceiling after the base shield rules are implemented.

Qualitative comparison

Defense plus prepared burst; no defensible numeric rank before a stat line, layered shields and encounter tests exist.

Proposal arithmetic & ranking limits

Nerielle Wavecrest

A Passive multiplies her proposed 18% recipient-HP heal to 19.8%. Her A2 gives 8.8% immediately plus two 4.4% Regeneration ticks, totaling 17.6% if both ticks occur.

Roselle Vey

Her Ultimate's extra 35% Max-HP damage per enemy needs 70% of her Max HP in shields actually consumed on Roselle. Five party shields are not pooled across five recipients. The 8% post-cast self-shield cannot fund the same cast.

Isavelle Frostmere

B Ultimate grants each of five allies 15% of Isavelle's Max HP in separate recipient shields: 75% distributed coverage, not one 75% shield or an automatic Roselle cap.

No exact strength ranking is assigned: final stats, Speed, source-layer shield rules and implemented draft skills do not yet exist.

Read the limits before comparing
  • These are conditional team outcomes for the existing 29 heroes, not a global hero ranking or results for the eleven proposed kits.
  • Compare within a cohort. Four anchors stay fixed and only the fifth Back-row seat changes; the anchor teams differ between A and B.
  • All three final bosses are Radiant, with varying approach mobs. Cross-element strength has not been established.
  • Encounters were fixed after a five-seed pilot; those pilot seeds are also in the full run. This is not held-out prediction.
  • Twenty seeds leave wide uncertainty: even 20/20 wins has a Wilson 95% interval of about 84–100%.
  • The actual auto AI ran complete encounters or the 200-player-action cap. This does not measure individual DPS, healing or prevention across six hero turns.
  • Displayed enemy levels 120/74/43 use different formulas from hero level 20. Late-Campaign wins flag progression scaling for review rather than approving it.
  • Remaining HP is team HP divided by starting team Max HP; it excludes shields and does not measure total healing.
  • Action-cap runs remain incomplete, not wins or defeats. These results make no manual-targeting, online/PvP or Android-performance claim.

Encounter gates.

  • No first-pass generic boss action may remove a full-HP same-investment support in one noncritical neutral hit; if it does, reduce ATK or power before testing combinations.
  • A telegraphed heavy strike must allow one hero opportunity; do not use Speed or AI sequencing to skip the advertised answer.
  • Track effective HP, including actual healing and shielding. If recovery exceeds 20% of starting boss HP per three boss turns in the baseline test, lower the heal/guard budget before judging team DPS.
  • Start with one boss and zero adds; add up to two creatures only by reallocating the same total enemy HP/attack budget, never multiplying the baseline encounter by three.
  • Break remains Basic0/Active1/Ultimate2 plus advantage1 once per successful target/action. No reaction, multi-hit or DoT Break, and no exceptional bonus is selected here.
  • Require at least one successful clear by the straightforward baseline and one specialist alternative after integration; publish failures, median turns and worst-seed survival rather than a guaranteed success claim.

Wave carry checks.

  • Run current Core multiwave validation separately; this notebook does not establish it passed.
  • Check that HP, shields, charge, cooldowns and ordinary status duration carry without an extra tick. Private draft resources follow their expressly selected reset rule after those kits are implemented.
  • Do not replay battle-start passives, farm rewards per wave or copy defeated enemies into the next-wave queue.
